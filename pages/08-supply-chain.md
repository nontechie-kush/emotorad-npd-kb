## THE GLOBAL E-BIKE SUPPLY CHAIN — OVERVIEW

The e-bike supply chain is almost entirely China-rooted at the component level, with final assembly spread across Europe, Taiwan, and increasingly Southeast Asia. Understanding this structure is essential for e-motorad's sourcing strategy.

```
RAW MATERIALS (China, Congo, Australia, Chile)
         ↓
CELL MANUFACTURING (China — CATL, EVE, Ganfeng; Korea — Samsung SDI, LG)
         ↓
BATTERY PACK ASSEMBLY (China — Phylion, Greentech, etc.; some Europe)
         ↓
MOTOR MANUFACTURING (Germany — Bosch, Brose; Japan — Shimano, Yamaha; China — Bafang)
         ↓
COMPONENT MANUFACTURING (Japan — Shimano drivetrain; Taiwan — finishing; China — bulk)
         ↓
FRAME MANUFACTURING (Taiwan — premium; China — volume; Europe — niche)
         ↓
ASSEMBLY (Europe — for EU market; China — for export; India — for India market)
         ↓
DISTRIBUTION → DEALER / DTC → END USER
```

**Key structural fact:** A Bosch-powered European e-bike contains: German motor, Chinese battery cells, Chinese battery pack assembly, Japanese drivetrain components, Taiwanese frame (likely), European final assembly. Global interdependence is total.

| **Ganfeng** | China | LFP | Various | Growing | Major lithium processor; expanding into cells |

**Grade A vs Grade B cells:** Grade A cells (Samsung, LG, Panasonic, CATL, EVE) have consistent specification, are properly QC'd, and are what all credible e-bike packs use. Grade B/C cells (anonymous Chinese) are cheaper but have higher failure rates — the #1 cause of e-bike battery fires.

**The Kunshan Cluster (Jiangsu Province):** The Kunshan area near Shanghai is China's e-bike battery manufacturing hub. Phylion and dozens of smaller assemblers operate here. For European compliance (EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542), Kunshan suppliers are investing in documentation — but many still lag. This is the sourcing risk.

**The Jinhua Cluster (Zhejiang Province):** Second major cluster. Known for volume, competitive pricing. More budget-oriented than Kunshan.

### Frame Sourcing Strategy for e-motorad

**India B2B / entry product:** Source from Ludhiana cluster (Hero, Avon, or a Tier-2 Ludhiana supplier). Cheaper, faster, locally serviceable, builds the domestic supply chain story. 6061 Al is fine for fleet.

**India mid-premium gravel:** Requires hydroformed Al with battery integration channel. Indian suppliers currently cannot do this. Options: (a) Taiwan ODM with India assembly, (b) China premium ODM, (c) design in India, manufacture in China/Taiwan initially.

**Europe premium:** Taiwan ODM is the standard. This is what Canyon, Orbea, and Trek all do. Budget: $200–400 per frame for hydroformed Al depending on complexity.

### Gates Carbon Drive (Belt Drive)

For B2B fleet and urban premium products, Gates Carbon Drive is a serious consideration:

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Lifespan | 20,000–30,000 km (vs. 1,500–2,000 km for e-bike chain) |
| Maintenance | Zero lubrication needed |
| Noise | Silent |
| Weight penalty | ~50–100g vs chain |
| Cost premium | $80–150 more than chain drivetrain |
| Drivetrain requirement | Single-speed or internal gear hub (IGH) |

**For fleet:** Belt drive + Enviolo or Shimano Nexus IGH = near-zero maintenance drivetrain. The TCO argument is compelling: chain replacement every 2,000 km on an e-bike vs. belt lasting 10x longer.

## SECTION 6: CONNECTORS & WIRING HARNESS

Often overlooked but the #1 field failure point in e-bikes. Poor connectors = water ingress = shorts = fire risk.

| Standard | IP Rating | Used In | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------|-------|
| **XT60** | Not IP-rated | DIY/budget | High current; needs additional weatherproofing |
| **Higo connectors** | IP67 | Quality mid-range | Chinese standard; widely used in OEM assembly |
| **Anderson Powerpole** | IP67 | Some premium | Heavy-duty; used in US market |
| **Bosch connectors** | IP67 | Bosch system | Proprietary; part of Bosch ecosystem |

**Recommendation:** Use Higo IP67 connectors throughout for any India or Europe product. Costs ~$5–15 more per bike vs. generic. Field failure rate drops dramatically.

### What Must Be Imported

| Component | Source | Import Duty | Duty Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Motors (Bafang, Shimano, Bosch) | China/Japan/Germany | 15–25% + GST | Adds ₹5,000–25,000 per unit |
| Battery cells | China, Korea | 15% + 18% GST | Significant; LFP cells hit hardest |
| Battery packs (assembled) | China | 15–25% + GST | Major cost driver |
| Shimano drivetrain (GRX) | Japan (via distributor) | 15% + GST | Manageable |
| Hydraulic brakes (quality) | Japan/Taiwan | 10–15% + GST | Moderate |
| Forks (suspension) | Taiwan/China | 10–15% + GST | Moderate |

**Import duty on e-bike components is a significant headwind for India-made premium e-bikes.** A Shimano EP600RS motor at $250 OEM cost becomes ~$320 landed in India. A 420Wh battery pack at $160 becomes ~$210 landed. The total duty impact on a mid-premium India product can be ₹15,000–40,000 per unit.

**Mitigation paths:**
1. **SPECS scheme** (Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Electronic Components and Semiconductors) — check if e-bike components qualify for incentives
2. **PLI scheme** — Production Linked Incentives for advanced chemistry batteries manufactured in India
3. **Local battery pack assembly** — import cells (lower duty than assembled pack), assemble in India with local BMS sourcing

## SECTION 9: SUPPLY CHAIN RISKS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|----------|-----------|------------|
| China export restrictions (motors/batteries) | High | Medium | Dual-source; qualify Taiwan suppliers; stockpile |
| EU tariffs on China-origin e-bikes (17.4–35.4%) | High | Currently active | India or European origin manufacturing; component sourcing diversification |
| India import duty increase on components | Medium | Medium | Local battery pack assembly; lobby via industry associations |
| Battery cell quality failure (fire) | Very high | Low (with Grade A) | Only Grade A cells; third-party QC; BIS certification |
| Shimano/Bosch supply allocation (they prefer large OEMs) | Medium | High for startups | Build relationship early; commit to volume; start with Bafang, migrate |
| Single-source frame supplier risk | Medium | Low | Qualify 2 frame suppliers before production |
| EU Battery Regulation documentation non-compliance | High | Medium | Work only with certified Chinese suppliers; get documentation upfront |
| Currency volatility (INR/CNY/EUR) | Medium | High | Forward contracts; price in stable currency |

## SECTION 10: RECOMMENDED SOURCING ROADMAP FOR e-motorad

### Phase 1 (0–18 months): India market entry

**Motor:** Bafang M500 (mid-premium) or M600 (premium) — accessible, documented, good performance
**Battery:** Phylion or equivalent Kunshan supplier — Grade A NMC cells, smart BMS, IP65+
**Frame:** Ludhiana India for fleet product; Taiwan ODM for mid-premium gravel
**Drivetrain:** Shimano GRX 600 for gravel; Shimano Cues for fleet
**Assembly:** India (Bengaluru or Pune) — builds domestic content story

### Phase 2 (18–36 months): Europe market entry preparation

**Motor:** Upgrade to Shimano EP600RS — contact Shimano OEM program; requires ~500+ unit/year commitment
**Battery:** Validate EU Battery Regulation documentation from Chinese supplier; qualify Taiwan backup
**Frame:** Taiwan ODM partner with hydroformed Al + integrated battery capability
**Certification:** EN 15194 (EPAC), CE marking, EU Battery Regulation compliance
**Assembly:** Europe-assembled preferred for EU market positioning — target Netherlands or Poland assembly hub

### Phase 3 (36–60 months): Premium tier + Bosch access

**Motor:** Bosch Performance SX or CX — requires €5M+ volume commitment; needs European distributor partnership
**Battery:** Consider in-house battery pack assembly in India — qualified cells, proprietary BMS
**Frame:** Own design, Taiwan or European production
**Vertical integration:** BMS software development for fleet management (India B2B) and consumer app (Europe)
