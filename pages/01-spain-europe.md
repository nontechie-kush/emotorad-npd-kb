## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY FOR DECISION-MAKING

Your manager's gravel bike hypothesis is **validated by real market data**:
- Gravel e-bikes grew **+25% YoY in Spain in 2024** — one of the fastest-growing sub-segments
- Urban e-bike sales went **DOWN** in the same period — the market is rotating toward versatile bikes
- The dominant players (Orbea, Specialized, Canyon) all sell integrated-battery gravel e-bikes at **£3,899–£8,999**

- Spain's government subsidies for e-bikes are **regional only** — no national scheme. Madrid and Galicia are most active.

## 3. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK — SPAIN & EU

### EU Standard (applies to all EPAC bikes)
- **EN 15194** — EPAC standard (Electric Power Assisted Cycles)
- Max continuous motor power: **250W**
- Max speed of assistance: **25 km/h** (pedal-assist cuts off above this)
- Max weight: typically cited as 25kg limit in common usage (not legally mandated per se)
- No license, insurance, or helmet required for EPAC class
- CE marking required
- **Battery Regulation 2023/1542** (EU) — new battery regulation, impacts BMS documentation, recyclability labeling, carbon footprint declaration (relevant for future product certification)

### Spain-Specific
- Spain follows EU EN 15194 — no additional national classification for EPAC
- Import duties: Spain as EU member applies EU customs tariff; significant BCD on Chinese-origin e-bikes/components
- **Subsidy eligibility tech requirements:**
  - Motor ≤ 250W continuous
  - Assistance cuts off at 25 km/h
  - Battery ≥ 248Wh (Madrid requirement)
  - Lighting system required for some programmes
  - Some regions cap eligible retail price

## 5. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE — INTEGRATED-BATTERY E-GRAVEL BIKES

### Your Manager's Hypothesis: Gravel Bike + Internal Battery + City Adaptation
This is exactly what the top 3 European e-bike brands are doing. Here is the complete current competitive map:

---

### COMPETITOR 1: Orbea Denna (Spanish brand, Basque cooperative)
**Why this matters most:** Orbea is Spanish, sells direct and through dealers across Spain, and has built their entire e-gravel strategy around the integrated-battery concept.

| Model | Price (GBP) | Motor | Battery | Groupset |
|-------|-------------|-------|---------|---------|
| Denna M11E | £8,999 | Shimano EP801RS-Gravel | 420Wh | Top-spec |
| Denna M10I | £8,999 | Shimano EP801RS-Gravel | 420Wh | Di2 |
| Denna M30 | £5,199 | Shimano EP801RS-Gravel | 420Wh | GRX 1x12 |
| Denna M40 | £4,999 | Shimano EP801RS-Gravel | 420Wh | Cues 1x11 |
| Denna H30 | £3,899 | Shimano EP600RS-Gravel | 420Wh | GRX 1x12 |
| Denna H40 | ~£3,000 est. | Shimano EP600RS-Gravel | 420Wh | — |
| Denna H50 | ~£2,400 est. | Shimano EP600RS-Gravel | 420Wh | — |

**Motor insight:** Orbea uses **Shimano EP801RS** (high-power, 85Nm, gravel-specific tuning) for top models and **EP600RS** (60Nm, lighter) for entry. Both use 420Wh concealed battery.

**Key positioning:** "Joyful Adventure — Gravel-tuned assistance for the steepest climbs and longest rides. Wide tire clearance."

**Orbea Gain (e-road, not gravel):** Uses **Mahle X20** rear hub motor + 350Wh. £4,999–£8,999.

---

### COMPETITOR 2: Specialized Turbo Creo 2
**Positioning:** "Stealth e-bike for serious road/gravel riders who don't want to look like they're on an e-bike"

| Model | Price | Motor | Battery | Weight |
|-------|-------|-------|---------|--------|
| Turbo Creo 2 Comp | €6,500 | Specialized SL 1.2 (320W, 50Nm) | 320Wh | 14.55kg |
| Turbo Creo 2 Expert | €8,500 | Specialized SL 1.2 (320W, 50Nm) | 320Wh | 15.78kg |

**Motor insight:** Specialized's proprietary SL 1.2 motor — 320W peak, 50Nm torque (33% more than previous gen), 40% quieter. Lightest motor in segment.

**Key trade-off:** Smallest battery (320Wh vs 420Wh for Orbea) → shorter range, but lowest weight. Appeals to weight-conscious performance riders.

---

### COMPETITOR 3: Canyon Grail:ON / Grizl:ON
**Positioning:** "Direct-to-consumer e-gravel, value-for-money vs. premium brands"

| Model | Price | Motor | Battery | Torque |
|-------|-------|-------|---------|--------|
| Grail:ON CF 7 | ~€5,000 | Bosch Performance CX Gen4 | 500Wh | 85Nm |
| Grail:ON CF 8 | ~€5,300 | Bosch Performance CX Gen4 | 500Wh | 85Nm |
| Grizl:ON (2025 new) | $3,699–$5,499 | Bosch Performance SX Sprint | 400Wh | 55Nm |

**Motor insight:** Canyon chose Bosch CX (most powerful, 85Nm) for top models — more "assistance" feel vs. Specialized (subtler). Grizl:ON uses newer lighter SX motor.

**Key advantage:** Largest battery (500Wh) → longest range. Direct-to-consumer = lower price for spec.
